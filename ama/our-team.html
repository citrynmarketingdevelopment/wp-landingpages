Amelia Molloy’s Angels

The hearts behind the mission

Every member of our team shares one common purpose: walking alongside those facing infertility with compassion, understanding, and hope. Some of us have lived the journey ourselves, while others have been called to serve it—but together, we are committed to ensuring no family has to face infertility alone.

“It takes a village to create a family.”

Founder & Executive Director

Ashley Antongiovanni

Ashley Antongiovanni is the Founder and Executive Director of Amelia Molloy’s Angels. Since beginning her career in the medical field in 2006, Ashley has devoted her life to caring for others, developing a deep passion for advocacy, and compassion. Today, she combines her healthcare experience with nonprofit leadership to oversee the organization’s vision, fundraising, community partnerships, volunteer engagement, family grant program, support groups, comfort kits, and advocacy efforts, all with the goal of ensuring no couple has to face infertility alone.

Ashley and her husband, Michael, were married in 2020. After experiencing infertility and the heartbreaking loss of their daughter, Amelia, during pregnancy, they felt God calling them to transform their pain into purpose. Amelia Molloy’s Angels was founded in Amelia’s honor to provide the emotional, physical, financial, and spiritual support they wished had existed during their own journey. Through her legacy, the organization is breaking the stigma surrounding infertility, restoring hope, and helping families pursue their dreams of parenthood.

While Ashley is grateful for every milestone the organization has achieved, what means the most to her cannot be measured in numbers. Seeing couples find hope again, celebrating pregnancies and births, hearing someone say they finally feel understood, and witnessing a community rally around families in their darkest moments are the accomplishments she treasures most. She believes infertility is far more than a medical diagnosis, it touches every aspect of a person’s life and that every couple deserves to be met with compassion, dignity, and unwavering support.

Above all, Ashley believes hope is at the heart of everything Amelia Molloy’s Angels does. She is passionate about reminding couples that their story matters, that they are never alone, and that even in life’s greatest heartbreak, God can bring healing, purpose, and beauty from the ashes. It is her greatest privilege to carry on Amelia’s legacy by helping others find hope and, ultimately, the gift of family.

Driven by a deeply personal experience and guided by her firm foundation in Christ, Jenny McLean serves as President of Amelia Molloy’s Angels. Jenny works alongside the AMA board of directors, committees, and prayer team to minister to Kern County couples enduring the silent struggles and profound hardships of infertility.

For five years, Jenny and her husband Jim faced their own painful battle. After surviving several heartbreaking miscarriages and watching the doors close on multiple medical procedures, they surrendered their journey to God and opened their hearts to adoption. In a whirlwind of grace, they were chosen. Within just 10 days, they stood in a delivery room, witnessing the birth of their son, who is now 23 years old.

This profound miracle and testament of God’s faithfulness fuels her mission today. Combining real-world empathy with professional leadership, Jenny works to ensure no couple walks alone. Through AMA’s grants, community, and unwavering support here in Kern County, she pours out Christ’s love to assure that every hurting heart feels seen, held, and strengthened in their waiting.

I am Cathy Gonzales, Vice President and Spiritual Leader of Amelia Molloy’s Angels. I am employed by the Kern High School District as a System Application Specialist. Seeing my daughter choose to use her grief to support and encourage other families inspired me to walk beside her in this mission. I was born and raised in Bakersfield, CA, but I have had the privilege to call many places home during my college years.
