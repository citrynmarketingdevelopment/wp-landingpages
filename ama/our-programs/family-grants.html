Gift of Family Grants

When hopeful parents learn what treatment costs, the dream can start to feel out of reach. Our grants exist to close that distance, paid directly to your clinic, agency, or surrogacy professional so the next step is actually possible.

What the grant is

The purpose is simple: remove the price tag

The inability to conceive is stressful and emotional enough on its own. Then the estimates arrive, and for most families the number is not something a savings account absorbs. We established the Gift of Family Grant so that money is not the reason a family stops trying.

Grants can be awarded up to $25,000 when funding permits, and are distributed to the IVF, surrogacy, or adoption professionals you have chosen. Awards are held for up to a year, and invoices from your facility can be submitted to us for payment as costs come due.

Both the number of grants and the size of each one depend on what our community raises during the year. Typically awards are made every four months, though that can be more or less frequent.

What we provide

Whichever path to parenthood is yours

We do not tell families how to build their family. We help fund the route they have chosen.

Fertility treatment

IVF cycles, transfers, medications, and the clinical care that goes with them, paid to the fertility center you are already working with.

Gestational surrogacy

Agency and professional costs associated with pursuing surrogacy, sent directly to the agency coordinating your journey.

Adoption

Adoption-related expenses, paid to the adoption agency handling your placement so the process can keep moving forward.

Who may qualify

Eligibility, stated plainly

These are the requirements every applicant must meet. Meeting them does not guarantee an award, but not meeting them means we cannot consider the application.

  • Diagnosed with infertilityThe diagnosis must come from a licensed physician.
  • A citizen or legal permanent residentNatural-born citizens and green card holders qualify. Work and student visas do not.
  • A resident of Kern CountyYou will confirm residency with a copy of your driver’s license.
  • In good overall physical and mental healthConfirmed through the medical evaluation forms your physician completes.
  • Able to demonstrate financial stabilityIncluding basic health insurance for maternal and prenatal care, adequate living arrangements, and the means to support a child.
  • Applying in the current cycleOne application per couple per cycle. Applicants who were not selected previously are welcome, and expected, to reapply.
How to apply

Four pieces to submit

Applications are accepted during designated periods each year. Everything must be in by 11:59 pm on the closing date of the cycle you are applying to.

1

The application

Complete the application form in full and upload it to the Complete Application folder in our secure file submission drive.

2

A short video

Record five to fifteen minutes introducing yourselves and what a grant would mean to your family, then upload it to the video folder. It does not need to be polished, just honest.

3

Medical evaluation

Have a licensed physician complete the medical evaluation forms, then upload the completed documents to the Medical Evaluation folder.

4

Residency confirmation

Schedule a short appointment with us to show a copy of your driver’s license so we can confirm Kern County residency.

Ready to start, or have a question first?

Application forms, guidelines, the file submission drive, and the residency appointment link are sent to every applicant when a cycle opens. Contact us and we will get you what you need, walk you through the timeline, and answer anything the application does not cover.

Please make sure your application is complete before the closing date. Incomplete submissions cannot be reviewed in that cycle, though you are always welcome in the next one.

Grant questions

How recipients are chosen, where the money goes, and what happens if this cycle is not your cycle.

How are grant recipients selected?

A committee made up of our Board of Directors and members of our advisory committee reviews every application received during the cycle. Each member has the chance to share their thoughts about the applicants with the rest of the committee, and an anonymous poll determines the top candidates.

Based on the funding available, the Board of Directors then approves how many grants will be awarded and the amount of each one.

How much is awarded, and how many grants are given?

Both depend on how much is raised during the year and on the type of treatment each family requires. Grants can go up to $25,000 when funding permits. Awards are held for up to a year so the money is there as your costs come due.

Where will my grant be sent?

To the fertility center, surrogacy agency, or adoption agency you have chosen, in your name. To protect funds that donors gave in trust, we cannot issue checks directly to recipients. As payments come due, a bill or invoice from your facility can be submitted to us and we will pay it.

Can I be reimbursed for something I already paid for?

No. So that we can be certain grant funds are used as intended, we are not able to issue reimbursements for payments already made. Payments are made directly to your clinic or agency on your behalf going forward.

What counts as a permanent resident of the United States?

Natural-born citizens and immigrants holding a green card. Work visas and student visas do not qualify as permanent residency for this program.

I was not selected last cycle. Do I apply again?

Yes, please do. Applicants must submit a new application for each cycle, and being passed over once does not count against you. Please limit yourself to one application per cycle.

What if I need help before the next cycle opens?

Tell us. Our Family Relief Fund is available year round for everyday bills, and our support groups, prayer team, and Comfort Kits are open to you at any point, grant or no grant.
