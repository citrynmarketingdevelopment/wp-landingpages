What a Comfort Kit is

For hard days A reminder that you are loved, supported, and never alone in your journey.

Infertility is not only a medical diagnosis. It is the quiet drive home after an appointment, the negative test you take alone in the bathroom, the surgery nobody at work knows about. Those are the days a Comfort Kit is made for.

Our free infertility comfort kits in Bakersfield, CA are assembled by community volunteers, filled with items chosen for rest and self-care, and hand-delivered or shipped to the person receiving it. There is nothing to fill out afterward and nothing expected in return.

We have given 310 Comfort Kits so far, and every one of them started with somebody telling us about a person who was hurting.

Who it is for

You do not have to be in crisis to deserve one

If any of these sound like you or someone you love, a Comfort Kit is meant for them.

  • Anyone facing infertilityNewly diagnosed, years in, or somewhere in between.
  • Recovering from a procedureEgg retrieval, a transfer, an IUI, or fertility-related surgery.
  • In the waitingThe two-week wait, a pending result, or a season with no answers yet.
  • Grieving a lossA failed cycle, a miscarriage, a stillbirth, or a placement that fell through.
  • Applying for a Gift of Family GrantEvery applicant receives a Comfort Kit, whether or not a grant is awarded.
  • Someone you loveYou can request a kit on another person’s behalf. Many of ours are sent that way.

Comfort Kit questions

If something is not answered here, just ask. No question is too small.

Does a Comfort Kit cost anything?

No. Comfort Kits are always free. They are funded entirely by donations and packed by volunteers, and there is never a cost, a copay, or an expectation of anything in return.

Can I request a kit for someone else?

Yes, and many of our kits are requested exactly that way. Send us their name, phone number, and mailing address. Tell us whether you would like the kit to come from you or simply from Amelia Molloy’s Angels, and we will honor that.

Do I have to be applying for a grant to receive one?

Not at all. Everyone who applies for a Gift of Family Grant receives a Comfort Kit, but the kits are open to anyone facing infertility. You do not need a diagnosis letter, an application, or any connection to our other programs.

What exactly will be in my kit?

Kits are assembled from donated items, so contents vary. Most include comfy socks, lip balm, a face mask, a journal, a candle, bath bombs, and a soft blanket, along with a handwritten note. If you have an allergy or a sensitivity, mention it in your request and we will adjust the kit.

How long does it take to receive one?

We assemble kits in batches, so timing depends on what we have on hand when your request comes in. We will confirm receipt and give you a realistic timeframe when we follow up. If the situation is urgent, say so, and we will do everything we can to move quickly.

Do you deliver outside Kern County?

Yes, of course! While our Family Grants focus on our local Kern County community, we send Comfort Kits to anyone who needs one, near or far.
