What Talking With Angels is

A room where you do not have to explain yourself

Talking With Angels is a co-ed group for individuals and couples living with infertility. It exists to provide emotional, informational, and social support, and to push back on how little the world understands about what this journey actually involves.

Our group is open to all and free of charge. We put out refreshments and we bring caring hearts, and the atmosphere is relaxed rather than clinical. Since it began in 2022 the group has grown steadily and produced a lot of genuine friendships along the way.

One thing worth knowing before you walk in: some attendees may already be pregnant. For many people that is an encouragement, proof that the story can turn. If it would be hard for you right now, tell us and we will talk it through honestly before you come.

Who it is for

Anyone carrying this, in any form

The group is for people dealing with infertility-related struggles and challenges. That is a wider circle than most people assume.

  • IndividualsYou do not need a partner with you. Plenty of people come on their own.
  • CouplesThe group is co-ed on purpose. Both partners are welcome and both are affected.
  • Anyone in treatmentIUI, IVF, medicated cycles, surgery, or waiting on a referral.
  • Anyone pursuing surrogacy or adoptionDifferent road, same waiting, same need for people who get it.
  • Anyone grievingMiscarriage, stillbirth, failed cycles, or a placement that fell through.
  • People on the other side of itMany who found their way through keep coming back, because they remember what it meant to have someone there.
What to expect

What the group is actually for

Six things Talking With Angels is built to do, in the words of the people who keep coming back.

Emotional support

A safe place for individuals and couples to say out loud how infertility actually feels. It cuts the loneliness down, which is most of what people come for.

Sharing information

Members trade what they have learned about treatments, clinics, insurance, and options, so decisions are made with real information instead of guesswork.

Understanding and empathy

Hearing someone else describe your exact experience does something no reassurance from the outside can do. It tells you nothing is wrong with how you are handling it.

Coping together

Practical talk about managing the emotional and logistical weight: the appointments, the announcements, the holidays, the questions from family.

Building community

Belonging, plainly. The group has produced a lot of friendships that continue well outside the room it meets in.

Raising awareness

Together the group speaks up about infertility in our community, which is how the stigma around it finally starts to break.

Support group questions

Mostly first-timer nerves. All of it is normal.

Does it cost anything to attend?

No. Talking With Angels is free and open to all. There is no membership, no fee, and no expectation that you donate, volunteer, or give anything back.

Do I have to talk?

Not at all. People come for their first few meetings and just listen, and that is completely welcome. The group is willing to listen when you need to talk, and it will not pressure you when you do not.

Can my husband or partner come?

Yes, and we hope they do. The group is co-ed by design. Partners carry this too, often with far fewer places to put it down.

Will there be pregnant people there?

Sometimes, yes. Some members have become pregnant during their time in the group and continue coming. For many attendees that is a source of real encouragement. If it would be painful for you right now, say so when you reach out and we will be honest with you about what to expect.

Is what I say kept private?

Yes. What is shared in the group stays in the group. That expectation is set out loud, and it is the reason people are able to be as honest as they are.

What if I have not been diagnosed yet?

Come anyway. The group is for anyone dealing with infertility-related struggles, and the months before a diagnosis are some of the most disorienting ones there are.
