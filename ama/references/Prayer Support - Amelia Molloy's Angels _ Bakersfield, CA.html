Prayer Support

 Would You Like Us to Pray For You?

At Amelia Molloy’s Angels, we understand that navigating infertility can be a deeply personal and challenging journey. We believe in the power of community and support, and we would be honored to lift you up in our thoughts and prayers.

We have a dedicated team continuously praying for those facing infertility and for our organization as a whole. Understanding firsthand how prayer sustained us during the most challenging moments, we felt compelled to establish a prayer team. Witnessing the transformative power of prayer is a profound expression of God’s love that inspires us every day. 

If you would like us to pray for you, please let us know. We are here to offer spiritual support and hope during this time. Please share your prayer request here:

Meet Our Prayer Leaders

We feel so blessed and honored to serve as prayer leaders for Amelia Molloy’s Angels. From the beginning, we have seen God’s hand over this mission, guiding it, protecting it, and using it to bring hope to couples walking through infertility.

We believe deeply in the power of prayer and in a God who is near to the brokenhearted. It is a joy for us to lift up every family, every decision, and every step this ministry takes, trusting that He is the One who brings comfort, strength, and the courage to keep hoping.

Serving in this role is not just a responsibility, it is a calling we embrace with grateful hearts. We pray that everything Amelia Molloy’s Angels does continues to reflect God’s love, faithfulness, and compassion.

With Love,
Cathy & Monty Gonzales
