Optimax Consulting Services, established in 2011, boasts a powerhouse of industry professionals with decades of combined experience. Our team’s deep expertise spans consulting, implementation, support, and product management for Practice Management and Electronic Medical Records systems. We partner with a diverse range of practices nationwide, offering comprehensive revenue cycle management, consulting, and HR services to maximize revenue efficiency. We leverage our team’s experience, refined processes, and advanced technology to streamline practice operations and enhance revenue capture.

Over the years, we have expanded through several joint ventures and acquisitions, further enhancing our capabilities and experience. Today, our combined team has been transforming practices for over three decades. We continue to uphold a legacy of unparalleled expertise and dedication to our clients, ensuring their operations are streamlined and their revenue is optimized.
