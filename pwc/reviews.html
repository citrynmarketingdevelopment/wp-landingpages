Common Questions

About Our Reviews

Where do these reviews come from?
Every review on this page was written by a patient on our Google Business Profile. We reproduce them as written and link to the full, current set on Google so you can read them all, including any posted after this page was last updated.
Do you offer anything in exchange for a review?
No. We never offer discounts, gifts, or any other incentive in exchange for a review, and we do not ask patients to change or remove what they have written. Honest feedback is the only kind that is useful to the next patient.
Will my review reveal that I am a patient here?
A Google review is public and appears under whatever name is on your Google account, so please share only what you are comfortable making public. You are welcome to keep your review general and leave out any specific health details.
Can a review tell me whether treatment will work for me?
No. Reviews describe one person's individual experience and are not a prediction or promise of any particular outcome. The most reliable way to know whether we are a good fit for you is a psychiatric evaluation, where we can discuss your symptoms, history, and goals directly.
How do I share concerns instead of posting publicly?
If something about your care did not go well, we would genuinely like to hear it directly. Call our office at (760) 688-4080 or email info@psychwellnesscenter.com so we can look into it.
What should I do if symptoms feel urgent or unsafe?
If you are experiencing a medical emergency, thoughts of harming yourself or someone else, or feel unable to stay safe, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately. Online content should not replace emergency care.
