What We Do

Strategic Support at Every Stage of Your Project.

From early planning to final commissioning, SJO delivers the expertise and field presence owners rely on to reduce risk, maintain quality, and keep projects moving forward.

    We act as an extension of the owner’s team, providing independent oversight and advocacy throughout the project lifecycle.

  • QA/QC & Inspections

  • Construction Management

    Field leadership and coordination that drives safety, accountability, and alignment with project goals and schedules.

    Timely, accurate reporting and documentation that provides visibility and supports informed owner decision-making.

    Planning, permitting, site readiness, and coordination to set the foundation for a successful project.

  • Commissioning Support

    Supporting the transition from construction to operations with testing, punch lists, and closeout oversight.
